Swipe or scroll within the links or anywhere across to the outlined dot. Arrow Up selects the item above; Arrow Down selects the item below. Enter follows the centered link. Escape closes navigation.

When closed, flick the navigation dot left or right to change sides. In portrait, flick up or down to move between bottom, middle and top. Each gesture moves along one axis only. In landscape, only bottom-left and bottom-right positions are available. With keyboard focus on the closed dot, use Left, Right, Up or Down Arrow. While open, swipe near the outlined dot to scroll links; tap it to close.
